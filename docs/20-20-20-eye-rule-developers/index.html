The 20-20-20 eye rule for developers

Long agent turns mean long stretches of staring at a screen. vibestretch includes eye breaks in its rotation, and 20-20-20 is one of them.

The 20-20-20 break in vibestretch

This is how the nudge reads:

🧘 20-20-20: look at something 20 feet away for 20 seconds.

Look at something 20 feet away. Keep looking for 20 seconds. Then go back to your agent.

Other eye breaks in the list

  • Blink 20 times, slowly.
  • Rub your palms warm, then cup them over your closed eyes for 20 seconds.
  • Stand up and walk to a window.

When it reminds you

vibestretch does not run on a clock. It reminds you while a long agent turn keeps you waiting, at most once every 15 minutes by default. Eye breaks come up as part of the rotation of twelve exercises. Read how the timing works in stretch breaks while AI coding agents run.

Only want eye breaks?

Write your own list with only the eye breaks you like. One exercise per line in ~/.config/vibestretch/exercises.txt. The built-in list then steps aside. See desk stretches for programmers for an example.

FAQ

Does vibestretch remind me every 20 minutes?
No. It has no fixed schedule. It reminds you only during long agent turns, and the default cooldown between nudges is 15 minutes.
Can I get only eye breaks?
Yes. Put your own exercises in ~/.config/vibestretch/exercises.txt, one per line, and vibestretch uses that list instead of the built-in one.
