Desk stretches for programmers

vibestretch ships twelve short exercises. Each one fits into the time an agent turn takes. They rotate, so you never get the same one twice in a row.

The twelve built-in exercises

  1. 20-20-20: look at something 20 feet away for 20 seconds.
  2. Stand up, reach for the ceiling and hold for 15 seconds. Then fold to your toes for 15 seconds.
  3. 10 squats.
  4. Slow neck rolls, 5 in each direction.
  5. Wrist circles, 10 each way.
  6. Blink 20 times, slowly.
  7. Shoulder blade squeezes, 10 times: pinch, hold 3 seconds, release.
  8. Posture check: ears over shoulders, shoulders over hips. Hold it.
  9. Stand up and walk to a window. Open it if you can.
  10. Rub your palms warm, then cup them over closed eyes for 20 seconds.
  11. Hip circles, 8 each way.
  12. One 4-7-8 breath: in for 4, hold for 7, out for 8.

Wrist circles and the posture check are there for people who think about RSI and posture during long days at the keyboard. vibestretch is a reminder. It does not diagnose or treat anything.

Use your own list

Put one exercise per line in ~/.config/vibestretch/exercises.txt. The built-in twelve then step aside. Blank lines and lines starting with # are skipped.

mkdir -p ~/.config/vibestretch
cat > ~/.config/vibestretch/exercises.txt <<'EOF'
# blank lines and comments are skipped
Refill the water glass. Yes, now.
Hang from the pull-up bar for 20 seconds.
Look out the window and find something green.
EOF

Set VIBESTRETCH_EXERCISES to another path to keep the file somewhere else. Per-project lists work that way too. The rotation goes through your list in order. A file with nothing usable in it falls back to the built-in list.

FAQ

How many exercises does vibestretch include?
Twelve: stretches, micro-workouts and eye breaks. They rotate so the same one never comes twice in a row.
Can I add my own stretches?
Yes. Write one per line in ~/.config/vibestretch/exercises.txt. Quotes and backslashes in your text are safe to use.
